# Brand Identity Checklist

This checklist helps designers review the basic structure of a brand identity system.

## Core elements

- Brand name
- Logo
- Symbol or mark
- Color system
- Typography
- Layout rules
- Image style
- Tone of communication

## Logo review

- Is the logo readable at small sizes?
- Is the logo usable in black and white?
- Is the logo consistent across digital and print media?
- Are clear space and minimum size rules defined?

## Color review

- Are primary and secondary colors defined?
- Are color codes documented?
- Is there enough contrast for readability?
- Are usage examples included?

## Typography review

- Are primary and secondary typefaces defined?
- Are heading, body, and caption styles clear?
- Is the typography system easy to apply consistently?
